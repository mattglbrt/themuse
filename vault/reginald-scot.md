Reginald Scot was the English writer [[james-i|James VI]] attacked by name in the preface to [[daemonologie]] as someone who "is not ashamed in publike print to deny, that ther can be such a thing as Witch-craft." The Muse stops here because he is the sceptic the king wrote against.

## Where the Muse Leads

- [[daemonologie]]: the king's treatise, whose preface names Scot as one of its two targets.
- [[johann-weyer]]: the other writer the preface attacks, in the same breath.
