The King James translators didn't use the Septuagint, "the Seventie" in their preface, as the base for their Old Testament. They worked from the Hebrew, and the preface says "The translation of the Seventie dissenteth from the Originall in many places," while still asking "yet which of the Apostles did condemne it?" Where printed Greek Bibles mattered was the Apocrypha: F. H. A. Scrivener (1884) says the second Cambridge company worked from the Aldine Greek Bible of 1518, the Roman Septuagint of 1586, and, for every book but 1 Esdras, the Complutensian, with 2 Esdras and the Prayer of Manasses done from the Latin.
