Johann Reuchlin met [[pico-della-mirandola|Pico]] in Italy in 1490. In 1494 he published *De verbo mirifico* at Basel, "his first publication on (Christian) Cabbala", and in 1517 his *De arte cabalistica* came out at Hagenau, dedicated to Pope Leo X. He's the second name on the Christian Cabala thread, after Pico.

## Where the Muse Leads

- [[christian-cabala]]: his two books carry it forward from Pico.
- [[pico-della-mirandola]]: the man he met in 1490.
