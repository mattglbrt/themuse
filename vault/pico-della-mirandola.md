Giovanni Pico della Mirandola was, in Brian Copenhaver's words, "the first Christian who described Kabbalah in detail or treated it as worthwhile". He had his 900 *Conclusions* printed in Rome at the end of 1486, and Innocent VIII indicted 13 of them. Copenhaver adds that the *Conclusions* "had nothing good to say" about a tradition called "Hermetic", which makes Pico the place to pull Christian Cabala and Hermeticism apart.
