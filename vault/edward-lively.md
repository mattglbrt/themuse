Edward Lively was Regius Professor of Hebrew at Cambridge, "unanimously elected" about May 1575, and he heads the first Cambridge company of King James translators on the standard list. He died in May 1605, early in the work; A. W. Pollard called his death "a great blow to the work". He's on this trail because his *Dictionary of National Biography* entry was checked for any cabalist, Hermetic, or occult learning, and it mentions none.

## Where the Muse Leads

- [[king-james-bible]]: his company had 1 Chronicles through Ecclesiastes, and he died early in the work.
