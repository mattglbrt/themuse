The Comma Johanneum is the passage at 1 John 5:7b-8a that first appeared in [[erasmus|Erasmus]]'s Greek New Testament in his third edition, of 1522 (Combs 1996). The usual story, which Combs cites to Metzger (1992), is that Erasmus had promised his critics he'd add it if one Greek manuscript turned up. That story is contested: Combs reports that "Henk J. de Jonge has recently demonstrated that nothing in Erasmus' writings indicates he formally made such a promise" (de Jonge, *Ephemerides Theologicae Lovanienses* 56, 1980).

## Where the Muse Leads

- [[erasmus]]: the editor who put it into his third edition, and the subject of the promise story.
- [[textus-receptus]]: the later name for the Greek line that starts with Erasmus's editions.
