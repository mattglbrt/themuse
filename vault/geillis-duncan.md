Geillis Duncan was a servant to David Seaton, deputy bailiff of Tranent. According to [[newes-from-scotland|Newes from Scotland]], he had her tortured "with the torture of the Pilliwinckes vpon her fingers," and under that torture she named others, beginning with [[agnes-sampson|Agnes Sampson]]. The pamphlet makes her the starting point of the [[north-berwick-witch-trials|North Berwick witch hunt]].
