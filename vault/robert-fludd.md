Robert Fludd was a London physician who, in print and in a manuscript addressed to [[james-i|James I]], defended the Rosicrucian brotherhood. The old *Dictionary of National Biography* heads his entry "rosicrucian". The Muse stops here because Fludd is the second documented meeting between the esoteric and James's court, after [[john-dee]]. Dee asked the king to clear his name. Fludd dedicated a book to him.

## Historical Context

The Rosicrucian manifestos first came out in print in 1614 and 1615 (see [[rosicrucian-manifestos]]). Fludd's defences of the brotherhood followed within two years. The *Catholic Encyclopedia* of 1912 names him, with Michael Maier, as the movement's defenders: "In the seventeenth century Michael Maier and Robert Fludd were its champions." That encyclopedia wrote about secret societies from a polemical Catholic position, so it's used here only for that plain statement, which the DNB bears out.

## Life & Work

**Physician.** The DNB entry begins "FLUDD or FLUD, ROBERT, M.D. (1574–1637), rosicrucian", and says "On 20 Sept. 1609 he was elected a fellow of the College of Physicians".

**How he came to the Rosicrucians.** "The movement was commended to Fludd's notice by the German alchemist, Michael Maier, who visited him in London" (DNB).

**The defences.** Two books, both printed at Leiden:

- "'Apologia Compendiaria, Fraternitatem de Rosea Cruce suspicionis … maculis aspersam, veritatis quasi Fluctibus abluens,' &c., Leyden, 1616"
- "'Tractatus Apologeticus integritatem Societatis de Rosea Cruce defendens,' &c., Leyden, 1617"

(both as the DNB gives the titles).

**To the king.** Two items tie Fludd to James. First, the DNB: "To a manuscript 'Declaratio breuis,' which he addressed to James I, are appended the confirmatory letters of French and German associates." The DNB doesn't give its date or where the manuscript is now. Second, his big work, the *Utriusque Cosmi … Historia*, printed at Oppenheim and Frankfurt from 1617 to 1624, "has two dedications, first to the Deity, secondly to James I" (DNB). Both points rest on the DNB alone and want the modern ODNB or the books themselves before an episode leans on them. A facsimile of *Utriusque Cosmi* is on archive.org, but the dedication hasn't been read in it yet.

## Claims & Disputes

**Not found: Fludd and Cabala.** The sources checked for this page don't tie Fludd to Kabbalah or Christian Cabala. The DNB article on him doesn't use the word. That's a gap in what's been read, not a finding that there's no connection.

## Where the Muse Leads

- [[rosicrucian-manifestos]]: the documents he defended in print, in 1616 and 1617.
- [[james-i]]: the king he wrote a Rosicrucian defence for, and the second dedicatee, after God, of his *Utriusque Cosmi*.
- [[john-dee]]: the other esoteric figure the record puts before James. Dee asked to be cleared and got no recorded answer; Fludd dedicated to him. There's no documented link between the two men, and the page doesn't claim one.
