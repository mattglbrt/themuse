The MIT License in `LICENSE` covers everything in this repository that
Matt Gilbert wrote: the site code, the scripts, the docs, and the vault
content (vault/**/*.md), including the notes, templates and bibliographies.

Not covered, because it is not ours to relicense:

- public/fonts/: Literata and EB Garamond, each under the SIL Open Font

- vault/assets/: images are public domain or third-party licensed. Each
  one's source and license are cited in the bibliography of the node that
  embeds it, and that license governs the image.
- Quoted passages from other works stay under their own terms. Short
  quotations appear with citation.
