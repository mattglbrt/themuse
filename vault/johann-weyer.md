Johann Weyer was the German physician [[james-i|James VI]] attacked, as "Wierus," in the preface to [[daemonologie]]: "The other called VVIERVS, a German Phisition, sets out a publick apologie for al these craftes-folkes." The Muse stops here because the king singled him out as a defender of the people he wanted punished.

## Where the Muse Leads

- [[daemonologie]]: the king's treatise, whose preface names Weyer as one of its two targets.
- [[reginald-scot]]: the English sceptic attacked alongside him.
