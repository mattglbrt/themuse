Giordano Bruno reaches this trail through [[frances-yates]]. Her publisher describes him as "both advanced philosopher and magician burned at the stake", and her 1964 book placed him "in the Hermetic tradition". Whether that tradition existed in the way she described is now disputed, which makes Bruno the test case for [[yates-thesis|the Yates thesis]].

## Where the Muse Leads

- [[frances-yates]]: her 1964 book is the reason Bruno sits on this map.
- [[yates-thesis]]: where her Hermetic reading of the Renaissance, Bruno included, gets weighed against later scholarship.
- [[hermeticism]]: the tradition she placed him in, and the argument over whether it was one.
